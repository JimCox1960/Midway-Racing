About

We'll Keep You On Track

Midway Racing was founded by Davant Latham in 2023. His love for the horse has been exemplified by a decades-long involvement in all facets of the Thoroughbred industry — from the sales ring to the shed row to the winner's circle.

He played polo at the University of Virginia and worked on racetracks and farms in the United States, New Zealand, and Australia before building a career on the management side of the business.

His past stops include General Manager of Vinery, Director of Bloodstock at Darby Dan Farm, Past President of the Kentucky Thoroughbred Farm Managers Club, active member of TOBA, and Board Member of the Thoroughbred Charities of America, alongside a number of successful pinhooking partnerships.

Aftercare

A Second Career for Every Horse

A horse doesn't stop being Midway Racing's responsibility the day it stops racing. Every horse that carries these colors is tracked for life — not just while it's earning purses.

01

Plan the exit before the entry

Every horse's next step — breeding stock, retraining for a second discipline, or retirement — is discussed with trainers and connections well before it's needed, not decided in a scramble.

02

Work with accredited placement partners

When a horse is ready to leave the track, Midway Racing places it through Thoroughbred Aftercare Alliance-accredited organizations equipped to retrain and rehome off-track Thoroughbreds responsibly.

03

Stay in touch after the sale

Midway Racing checks in on its horses after they've left the stable. If a placement ever falls through, the horse comes back to Midway — that commitment doesn't have an expiration date.
